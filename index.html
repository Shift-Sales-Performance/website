Common questions

Before you sign

What does the Pipeline Performance Review include?

We analyse your CRM data for progression rates, follow-up speed, deal ageing and stalled revenue, then present the findings and a one-page business case for your leadership team. The fee is credited against the program if you go ahead within 60 days.

What happens after the 12-week program?

Most clients continue with ongoing coaching, billed annually, and can move to the SHIFT Platform when it launches. There's no long-term lock-in.

Do we keep the CRM set-up if we stop?

Yes. The fields and dashboards stay in your CRM. The coaching, quarterly reviews, benchmarks and certification stop when your coaching ends.

Will my salespeople see this as surveillance?

SHIFT is built for coaching, not inspection. We keep CRM fields to the minimum and managers use the data to help sellers move deals.
